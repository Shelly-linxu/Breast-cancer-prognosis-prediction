Research use only

This estimate may support research and prognosis discussion. It must not be used alone to select treatment, alter follow-up, or replace clinical judgement.

Model information

What this estimate means

The calculator estimates the probability of death from any cause within five years of breast cancer diagnosis. It reflects outcomes and treatment patterns observed in the Guangzhou Breast Cancer Study; it does not estimate the effect of a specific treatment.

Who the model is for

Women aged 19–97 years with newly diagnosed invasive breast cancer, stages I–IV. It has not been evaluated for men, carcinoma in situ, recurrent disease, or prediction after treatment has begun.

Predictors and missing information

The model requires age at diagnosis, AJCC overall stage, ER, PR, HER2, and Ki-67. A prediction is not produced if any value is unknown. HER2 equivocal is an explicitly supported model category.

Development and validation

The model was developed using 4,231 women with invasive breast cancer diagnosed between 1 October 2008 and 31 January 2018. Internal validation included bootstrap optimism correction and temporal validation among women diagnosed from 2015 onward. Independent external validation and clinical-impact evaluation remain necessary.

Technical details and limitations

This browser implementation averages predictions from 10 Cox-model fits created after multiple imputation. Age is represented with a natural cubic spline with knots at 43 and 52 years. Limitations include development in a regional cohort, potential temporal and treatment-policy dependence, and evidence of non-proportional hazards in model diagnostics.
